Introduction

Welcome to Cheerful Music, an AI-powered music creation platform operated by Cheerful Music Limited, a company registered in England and Wales.

At Cheerful Music, we believe that Everyone Can Be a Singer. Our platform enables people to create, save and share music through AI-powered tools, even without prior musical experience.

These Music Publishing & Community Guidelines govern public music publication, artist identities and profiles, community discovery and charts, content moderation, and related activities on the Cheerful Music platform. By submitting music for public publication, users agree to comply with these Guidelines, our Terms of Service, and applicable laws.

Scope and Relationship to Other Terms

These Guidelines govern public submission, discovery, artist profiles, community charts and content moderation on Cheerful Music (the “Platform”). They supplement the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, AI Music Rights & Licensing Terms, and any separate distribution or cover licence. Where laws give users non-waivable rights, those laws prevail.

1. Our Mission

Everyone Can Be a Singer. Cheerful Music helps people turn ideas, experiences and emotions into songs without requiring professional music experience. Our community is designed to support responsible creation, discovery and sharing.

2. Create, Save and Publish

Users may create and save music privately, share it using permitted tools, or submit it for public posting. Songs are private by default unless the user deliberately selects public posting. Public submission is subject to proportionate automated and/or human checks.

Posting on Cheerful Music is distinct from distribution to Spotify, Apple Music or other outside services. Availability of paid generation, exports or commercial use depends on the applicable service and upstream licence.

3. Artist Identity and Profile

Users may select an artist display name, image and public profile. A display name does not guarantee exclusivity or reserve a third-party streaming-artist profile. Do not impersonate artists, companies or other users. We may request changes where identity misuse or rights infringement is reasonably identified.

Account identifiers and public artist names are separate: changing a display name does not transfer ownership of historical user content or erase legitimate enforcement records.

4. Ownership, Licences and User Contributions

Copyright status and ownership of AI-generated material depend on jurisdiction, the human creative contributions involved, underlying source rights and applicable generation-provider terms. Generation or publication does not by itself create copyright or transfer all rights to Cheerful Music.

To the extent the user owns or controls the relevant rights, publicly posting content grants Cheerful Music a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, technically reproduce, stream, display, moderate and promote the posted content within Platform services and official discovery channels, including through service providers acting on our behalf. This licence is limited to those functions and ends when content is removed, subject to reasonable caching, backups and legal retention.

Any assignment, exclusive licence, exploitation outside the ordinary Platform service, royalty split or external distribution must be separately and validly agreed by the rights holder. Nothing in these Guidelines purports to grant rights not legally held by the user or Cheerful Music.

5. AI Content Transparency

Songs may be labelled “AI-generated”, “AI-assisted” or “Human-performed”, as appropriate to actual provenance and applicable law. Users must not falsely represent AI voices as real performers or conceal a material voice or identity impersonation. We may preserve provenance metadata and apply additional disclosures where legally required.

6. Unacceptable Content

Content may be rejected, restricted or removed where it contains or promotes the following:

  • Copyright, trademark, sample, backing track or image infringement; unauthorised voice cloning or identity impersonation.
  • Child sexual abuse material; sexual exploitation of minors; non-consensual intimate or sexual deepfakes.
  • Credible threats, targeted harassment, bullying, unlawful hate or violent incitement.
  • Disclosure of another person’s private information without appropriate authorisation.
  • Fraud, malware, spam, fake engagement, automated chart manipulation, or other illegal activity.

7. Covers, Remixes and Catalogue Songs

A cover, remix, sample, adaptation or use of an existing master recording requires the rights and permissions applicable to that use. Where Cheerful Music expressly offers songs from its catalogue for cover creation, separate Cheerful Cover Licensing Terms will state permitted arrangements, master/backing-track use, public posting, territories, revenue and external distribution restrictions.

A Platform cover permission does not automatically confer rights to exploit a cover commercially or distribute it through outside streaming services.

8. Recommendations, Rankings and Fair Play

Public songs may be eligible for discovery, playlists and charts based on valid streams, engagement, editorial decisions and safety/fraud checks. No user is guaranteed chart position, play count, exposure or income. Artificial traffic, purchases of engagement, bot activity and coordinated ranking manipulation are prohibited.

We may restrict recommendation eligibility, disclose major ranking factors, and offer reporting or review routes as applicable by law.

9. Global Music Distribution

Global distribution is a separate service, if offered, under a separate agreement, pricing and eligibility review. It may require rights verification, AI-provenance disclosures, metadata, identification, clearances, payments and royalty terms. Outside distributors and streaming platforms make independent acceptance decisions.

Cheerful Music does not guarantee acceptance, permanence, monetisation, playlists, streams or royalties on any outside service.

10. Privacy and Public Visibility

A public artist name, profile and posted song may be visible globally and may be indexed or shared. Do not publish personal details, addresses, schools, contact details or sensitive information about anyone, particularly children. Details of data collection, retention, access rights and third-party processing belong in the Privacy Policy.

11. Reports, Review and Appeals

Users and affected persons may report unlawful, harmful, infringing or impersonating content. We may remove content, restrict visibility, add labels, temporarily suspend features or terminate accounts using proportionate review processes. We will provide notifications, reasons and appeal opportunities where required by applicable law.

High-risk child-safety reports should be prioritised and escalated to trained reviewers and relevant authorities where required.

12. Copyright Notifications

Copyright holders may send notices to the copyright contact identified on the Platform. Notices should identify the work, allegedly infringing URL or song, contact details, authority and relevant statements required by law. Where applicable, counter-notification and appeals are available. Additional country-specific notice procedures may apply.

13. Edits, Removal and Retention

Users may change available privacy controls or remove songs from the Platform subject to existing legal obligations. Deletion cannot necessarily reverse independently shared copies, caches or external distribution; the latter must be managed under separate distribution terms.

14. No Guarantee of Copyright or Commercial Rights

AI-generated works may not qualify for copyright in every jurisdiction. Download, generation and public posting alone do not confer ownership, exclusivity, export eligibility or third-party commercial permission. Users should review the particular rights granted for the generation tool, territory and release service.

15. Protection of Minors / Child Safety

15.1 Eligibility, age declaration and service model

Account registration, AI generation purchases and public music publishing are available only to users aged 18 or older. user experience. This is a proposed product policy, not a statement that all jurisdictions legally require an age of 18. Users must give accurate age information, and we may deploy proportionate age-assurance measures where required. We will not knowingly permit under-18 users to evade applicable restrictions.

Where a law requires additional restrictions, consent, or an approved youth experience, the stricter legally applicable safeguards will govern. A self-declared age gate alone may not satisfy every obligation; service accessibility by children must be separately assessed.

15.2 Children under 13 and parental consent

The Platform is not intentionally directed to children under 13 under these Guidelines. We will not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children under 13 without a lawful basis and, where COPPA applies, the required notice and verifiable parental consent or a valid exception. If we learn that an under-13 child has provided personal information improperly, we will restrict relevant features and take appropriate deletion or remediation steps in accordance with law.

15.3 Ages 13–17 and regional requirements

Ages 13–17 are minors under these Guidelines and cannot create an account or publicly publish until a supervised youth programme is implemented. In the EU/EEA, the threshold for a child’s consent to processing based on consent for an information-society service can vary by Member State between 13 and 16; this does not override other applicable child protections. In the UK, age-appropriate design considerations may cover users under 18. In China, processing information of children under 14 carries guardian-consent and special-rule requirements. The Platform applies relevant local age eligibility and child-protection requirements.

15.4 Safety-by-design and default privacy

If minors are ever permitted, their profiles, songs, voices, contact details and activity should be private by default, with public sharing and discoverability disabled unless a lawful, documented age-appropriate path is implemented. Default direct messages, public comments from strangers, location sharing and searchability should be restricted by risk. Do not publicly display date of birth, school, contact details or precise location. Apply data minimisation, appropriate retention periods and heightened security.

15.5 Voice, likeness and AI impersonation

No person may upload, clone, transform or commercially exploit a minor’s voice, likeness or performance without all required lawful permissions. Consent by a parent or guardian alone may not cure an otherwise unlawful, exploitative or harmful use. We may require additional verification and prohibit deceptive or sexualised voice and image impersonation. A separately documented voice-consent flow is required before any voice-cloning feature is enabled.

15.6 Content moderation and child exploitation

We prohibit child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexual exploitation, predatory contact, coercion, bullying, threats, and encouragement or instructions for self-harm or eating disorders. Reports involving imminent risk to a child are prioritised. We may preserve legally required evidence and report to competent authorities or relevant reporting bodies where law requires or permits.

15.7 Recommendations, charts and ads

If minors are enabled in future, recommendations, charts, engagement features and commercial prompts must be risk-assessed for age appropriateness. Avoid incentives that pressure children to reveal information or remain online excessively. Where the EU Digital Services Act applies, online platforms must not use profiling-based targeted advertising toward users known with reasonable certainty to be minors. Additional local advertising restrictions may apply.

15.8 Purchases, credits and professional services

Under this 18+ policy, minors cannot independently buy AI credits, request paid public distribution or enter high-end recording and production contracts through the Platform. A future youth offering would require verified lawful consent and appropriate adult-managed payment arrangements, transparent pricing, refund processes and safeguards against excessive spending. No minor is promised professional representation or commercial release.

15.9 Parents’ and guardians’ requests

Parents and lawful guardians may report an underage account, safety concern, unauthorised use of a child’s voice or image, or request review, deletion or restriction of relevant personal information through the child-safety reporting channel identified on the Platform. We will assess requests and identity/authority through proportionate, privacy-respecting means and respond within legally required periods.

15.10 Reporting channels and transparency

The Platform will provide accessible child-safety reporting and, where required, complaint/appeal mechanisms, explain major safety measures in user-facing language, and conduct regional child-access and safety risk assessments before enabling relevant community features. Automated moderation may be supplemented with trained human review.

16. Policy Changes and Contact

We may update these Guidelines with appropriate notice. Material changes requiring consent will be handled under applicable law. Governing law, dispute resolution, contract entity and local mandatory consumer protections should be stated in the separate Terms of Service.
