This Policy describes the personal information we collect, why we use it, when it is shared, how it is protected, and the rights available to you. It applies to our website and related AI music creation, account, credit, publishing and community services (the “Services”). Certain optional future services will be covered by additional notices before they are introduced.

1. Who We Are and Scope

CHEERFUL MUSIC LIMITED (“Cheerful Music”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is generally the controller of personal data collected through the consumer Services. If we process personal data only on a business customer’s instructions, the business customer may be the controller and separate terms may apply.

This Policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service (including Credits, Payments and Refunds) and Music Publishing & Community Guidelines. It does not grant a copyright licence or replace any separate distribution, cover-song or professional production agreement.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account and identity information: email address, telephone number, authentication identifier, account ID, login method, password credential where used, age or age-eligibility information, settings, preferences, and support communications.
  • Artist and community information: artist name, profile photo, biography, public songs, covers, artwork, publication choices, comments, likes, follows, reports, moderation actions and chart activity.
  • Creative inputs and outputs: prompts, personal stories and other text you submit, lyrics, music preferences, uploaded media, images and audio, generated lyrics and songs, generation history and creative project metadata.
  • Payments and credits: packages, order and transaction identifiers, purchased and promotional credit balances, credit use, refund status, billing details and anti-fraud signals. Payment providers may directly collect full card details; we ordinarily receive payment confirmations, tokens and limited transaction metadata.
  • Technical and activity information: IP address, approximate region derived from network data, device/browser characteristics, access logs, pages and features used, generation and playback events, referral source, session identifiers and fraud/security events.
  • Third-party login information: limited details supplied by Google, Apple, WeChat or other identity providers when you choose those options, subject to their permissions and your settings.

Please do not submit other people’s private or sensitive personal information in song prompts unless you have a lawful basis and the necessary permission. A personal story or voice recording may itself contain sensitive information.

3. Information From Other Sources

We may receive authentication details from your chosen sign-in provider, transaction confirmation from payment processors, abuse or fraud signals from security services, and copyright claims or reports from rights holders or other users. The information received depends on the services actually enabled.

4. How and Why We Use Information

Depending on the processing activity, our legal bases under UK GDPR and, where applicable, EU GDPR may include performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests (subject to your rights), and consent. We will not treat consent as a substitute for a required lawful basis.

  • Provide the Services and perform our contract: create and secure accounts, generate requested lyrics and music, store private projects, deliver purchased credits, handle payments and support requests.
  • Operate the public community: publish content only when you select public publishing, display artist profiles, operate discovery and rankings, moderate content and respond to reports.
  • Maintain integrity and security: prevent repeated promotional-credit claims, account takeover, spam, payment fraud, illegal content and ranking manipulation; investigate incidents and enforce platform rules.
  • Comply with law: maintain required records, respond to valid legal requests, address intellectual property complaints and manage consumer or privacy-rights requests.
  • Improve the Services: conduct reliability testing, understand aggregate feature performance and improve interface design. Any optional use of identifiable private creative content for model training or materially different AI development requires separate assessment and transparent disclosures, with consent where required.
  • Marketing: send promotions where we have a lawful basis and provide opt-out options. Service, payment and security notices may still be sent as necessary.

5. AI Generation and Third-Party Model Providers

To generate lyrics and music, we may transmit the prompts, text, requested settings and related content necessary for the task to external AI service providers, including our selected text and music generation providers. Those providers may process the information in other countries and under their applicable contractual arrangements. We do not represent that creative prompts remain exclusively on Cheerful Music servers.

Users should avoid entering confidential information or sensitive details that they do not want processed by such providers. The specific processors, retention settings, international transfers and any provider model-training uses must be checked against the production configuration and disclosed in the applicable provider information before launch.

Where voice cloning, voice conversion or other voice-model features are introduced, we will provide a separate notice describing the data, purpose, retention and consent or other legal basis, including any biometric-data implications. These features are not described as currently available under this Policy.

6. Private Projects, Public Publishing and Rankings

Songs and projects should remain private unless you affirmatively choose to publish them. If you publish a song, the selected song, artwork, public artist name and associated profile details may be accessible to other users and possibly to people outside the Services. Public activity may affect recommendations and charts; ranking systems may use valid engagement and abuse-detection signals.

Removing public content may not remove copies already accessed, shared or cached by third parties. Global distribution to external streaming platforms is a separate service that requires additional disclosures and agreements.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential technologies for log-in, security, preferences and platform operation. If non-essential analytics, advertising or personalisation technologies are enabled, we will provide the required choice mechanism and additional information about their purposes and providers. Where consent is required, such technologies will not be activated before valid consent. You may manage preferences through an available settings mechanism and your browser.

7.1 Site Usage Analytics

We collect anonymous, server-side usage events (page views, dwell time and similar interaction signals) to understand how the Services are used and to improve them. These events are aggregated and are never used to identify you individually. To produce a coarse, aggregate country distribution, we derive your country from your device’s IP address at the time of the request using an offline geolocation database. We store only the derived country name (for example, “China” or “United States”) and do not retain your raw IP address. Aggregated analytics are processed only when analytics collection is enabled on our servers, and access to analytics dashboards is restricted to authorised accounts.

8. When We Share Information

  • AI generation and technical providers, cloud hosting, storage, authentication, email/SMS, security and customer support services, only as needed for the relevant functions and subject to appropriate arrangements.
  • Payment processors, fraud-prevention services and financial providers for payments, tax, dispute handling and anti-fraud measures.
  • Other users and the public when you deliberately publish a song, artist profile, comment or other public material.
  • Music distributors, professional studios and producers if you separately request those services and the relevant information sharing is explained.
  • Regulators, courts, law enforcement, rights holders or professional advisers where lawful and necessary, and parties to a corporate transaction subject to appropriate protections.

We will not describe personal data as “sold” or “not sold” for a particular jurisdiction without confirming our actual advertising, analytics and partner arrangements. If a statutory opt-out applies, we will provide it.

9. International Data Transfers

Because the Services are global and use external technology providers, personal data may be accessed or processed outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area. Where required, we will use a recognised adequacy decision or suitable transfer safeguards (such as applicable standard contractual clauses and UK transfer mechanisms), and conduct any necessary transfer risk assessment. Further details may be requested using our privacy contact channel.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes stated here, including account operation, user-selected storage, tax and financial records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security and legal compliance. Retention periods depend on the data category and operational need. We will establish and publish a category-based retention schedule before launch, rather than inventing uniform periods. Data may be deleted, anonymised or securely retained where a legal exception applies.

11. Security

We use organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to risk, which may include access control, encryption in transit, logging, provider oversight and incident response processes. No online system is completely secure. Please protect your sign-in credentials and report suspected unauthorised account access promptly.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and verification, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data; request data portability; withdraw consent where relevant; and complain to a supervisory authority. You may also have rights relating to certain automated decisions. UK users may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office; EEA users may contact the relevant local supervisory authority.

Residents of certain U.S. states may have additional rights, including rights to know, delete, correct, obtain information about certain disclosures, and opt out of qualifying sale, sharing or targeted advertising where applicable. We will not discriminate unlawfully for the exercise of privacy rights.

Please direct requests to the privacy contact listed at the start of this Policy. We may verify identity and respond within the period required by applicable law.

13. Automated Fraud Detection and Content Moderation

We may assess unusual registration, device, network, payment and credit-claim patterns to identify suspected fraud, including repeated new-user reward claims. This does not mean we can reliably identify the same individual across all email addresses or devices. We should not take solely automated decisions producing significant legal or similar effects without satisfying applicable requirements, and users should have an appropriate route to contest adverse actions.

14. Children and Age Eligibility

Our intended initial consumer account and payment offering is for individuals aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly solicit registration from children for this offering. Where we learn that personal data has been improperly collected from a child, we will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

An 18+ eligibility statement alone does not eliminate applicable obligations for services likely to be accessed by children. We will assess appropriate age assurance, protective defaults, public-sharing risks and children’s data safeguards before launching and as the Services evolve. If we later permit minors, we will update our processes and notices, including parental involvement where legally required.
